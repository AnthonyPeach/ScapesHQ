End to end

One line of work, eight stages.

A lead enters, the team works it, it becomes a customer, the work becomes a project, pricing becomes a proposal, the homeowner signs, and the record keeps everything that happened along the way.

01 · Intake

Lead lands

A Facebook Lead Ad submission arrives and routes to the correct workspace by connected Page. Calls and referrals are entered once, with source captured.

02 · Qualify

Team works it

Staff review, contact and qualify the lead. Follow-ups, assignments and every touch stay on the record instead of in someone's head.

03 · Convert

Relationship forms

The lead becomes a client, with contacts, company and property linked into the structure the real customer actually has.

04 · Pipeline

Project opens

The opportunity moves through the stages your business defines, and each stage change is recorded historically.

05 · Price

Estimate built

Sections, line items and saved templates produce the scope and totals without rebuilding the same job from scratch.

06 · Issue

Proposal sent

Options, attachments and templates go out to the homeowner. Send history is tracked, so a failed send is reconciled rather than repeated.

07 · Accept

Homeowner signs

The customer reviews on a page built for them and accepts, with acceptance and signature evidence stored on the proposal.

08 · Execute

Job runs

Job details and cost items sit under the project, and notifications push the events the team needs to act on.

When it goes wrong

The failure cases are handled, not hidden

Most of the damage in a contractor's software stack happens in the gaps: a lead that never arrived, a proposal sent twice, a retry that duplicated a record. These are the parts we built on purpose.

A lead fails to import

The intake event is recorded with its failure state and can be retried or replayed. The original event is not thrown away.

A Page connection breaks

Connections report as active, revoked, error or expired, so a dead integration surfaces instead of quietly costing you a month of leads.

A proposal email is ambiguous

Send history is tracked and reconciled rather than blindly re-fired, so the customer doesn't get the same proposal twice.

Two people act at once

Safeguards protect against duplicate processing and conflicting operations on the same record.

A workspace closes

Closure blocks future activity and safely shuts down connected systems, including disabling the Facebook Page integration when appropriate.

You want out

Data export and account closure are controls inside the product, not a support ticket and a two-week wait.

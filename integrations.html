Connected systems

It plugs into where your leads and your team already are.

The Facebook integration is the deep one — live in production, with connection health, routing and recovery built around it. The rest deliver mail, notify the team and keep the platform running.

Live in productionDeepest integration

Facebook / Meta Lead Ads

Connect your Facebook Page and lead ad submissions arrive inside ScapesHQ automatically, routed to the correct business workspace by the Page they came from. The connection is monitored, and intake that fails can be recovered instead of disappearing.

What the integration does

  • Facebook login and Page connection
  • Page selection for the workspace
  • Automatic lead intake from Lead Ads
  • Routing to the correct business workspace
  • Connection health monitoring
  • Disconnect and connection lifecycle management
  • Encrypted storage of credential information
  • Automatic cleanup when a workspace is permanently closed

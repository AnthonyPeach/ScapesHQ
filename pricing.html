Straight answers

The questions contractors actually ask

Do I pay per lead?

No. Bring in as many leads as your ads produce — the price is for the workspace, not the volume.

What happens to my data if I leave?

Data export is a control inside the product, and account and workspace closure belong to the owner. You don't have to ask permission to go.

Can I collect payment through it?

Not yet. Invoicing and payments are reserved in the platform but not shipped, and we won't sell you a screenshot of them. See the features page for the full built-versus-reserved list.

Do you help me get set up?

Yes. Connecting your Facebook Page, defining your pipeline stages and building your first estimate templates is part of onboarding, not an upsell.

Connect your Page, take one real inquiry from intake to a sent proposal, and look at the activity record it leaves behind. Twenty minutes, no slides.

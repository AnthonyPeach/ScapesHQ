Fit

For contractors who sell the work and then have to do it.

ScapesHQ is built for outdoor contracting companies with a few employees inside the same business — the ones where the person who answers the phone, the person who prices the job and the person who digs it are three different people.

Drainage

Yard drainage, regrades, trench drains, catch basins and outlets. Long scopes, a lot of photos, and a homeowner who needs it explained before they'll sign.

Hardscape

Patios, walls, walkways and outdoor living. Multi-option proposals, material specs, and estimates you shouldn't be rebuilding from a blank page every time.

Landscape

Install and renovation work with repeat customers, multiple properties per client, and a history worth keeping between visits.

The seats

Everyone in the business, in the same records

Each company operates inside its own isolated workspace, and roles decide reach — so the office isn't guarding a spreadsheet from the sales guy.

Owner

Full control of the workspace, team, integrations, business settings and account lifecycle.

Admin / Manager

Manages leads, customers, jobs, proposals, integrations and the team's workflow.

Sales

Works leads and follow-ups, builds estimates and quotes, sends proposals.

Office

Customer information, documents, scheduling-related workflow and general operations.

You'll get value on day one if

  • You run Facebook lead ads and leads land in a form inbox nobody watches
  • Your estimates live in a spreadsheet and your proposals live in a PDF folder
  • You've sent the same customer two versions of the same proposal
  • You have repeat customers with more than one address
  • Somebody in the office has to ask somebody in the field what happened on a job
  • You've lost a job because the follow-up never got made

You should wait if

  • You need invoicing and payment collection inside the same tool today
  • You need crew time tracking and job actuals against estimates
  • You need SMS to customers from the platform
  • You need custom fields or automated playbooks to model your process

Those are on the plan, and named on the features page as reserved rather than shipped. If one of them is a dealbreaker, tell us on the walkthrough and we'll be straight about timing.

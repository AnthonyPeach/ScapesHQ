01 · Leads & intake

Every inquiry lands in the same place

Facebook Lead Ads flow in automatically and route to the correct workspace by connected Page. Phone calls, referrals and yard signs get entered once, with their source recorded, so you can tell later what actually produced work.

  • Automatic Facebook Lead Ads intake, routed by Page
  • Manual lead entry with source, contact details and intake notes
  • Lead status and stage, defined by your business
  • Follow-ups and next actions attached to the lead
  • Full activity history on every lead record
  • Intake state tracked: received, processing, created, failed, rejected
  • Failed intake can be retried or replayed instead of lost

02 · Customers & properties

A customer is a structure, not a row

Most CRMs flatten a household or a property manager into one contact line. ScapesHQ keeps clients, contacts, companies and properties as separate records that link together, so the real relationship survives contact with reality.

  • Clients, contacts, companies and properties as connected records
  • Multiple contacts on one client — the caller and the signer
  • Multiple properties under one client or company
  • Property-level history that outlives a name change
  • Relationships between records, not duplicated rows
Example · relationship
  • Company — Ridgeview Property Group
  • Client — Whitfield household
  • Contacts — Dana Whitfield (calls), Marcus Whitfield (signs)
  • Properties — 1408 Ridgeview Ct · 22 Cedar Bend · 905 Fern Hollow
  • History — 3 projects, 5 proposals, 1 open job
03 · Projects & jobs

The pipeline is your pipeline

Projects are opportunities moving through stages you define. Jobs are the work being performed. Both stay tied to the customer and property they came from, and stage changes are kept as history rather than overwritten.

  • Business-defined pipeline stages
  • Stage history recorded on every project
  • Projects linked to the client, contacts and property
  • Jobs with job details and job cost items
  • Board, table and detail views of the same records

04 · Estimates & quotes

Price it once, reuse it forever

Estimates are built from sections and line items, and saved templates mean the fifteenth trench drain doesn't get rebuilt from a blank page. Totals and scope carry straight into the proposal.

  • Estimate sections and line items
  • Reusable estimate templates
  • Pricing totals calculated on the estimate
  • Scope information carried into the proposal
  • Quotes tracked alongside the project they belong to

05 · Proposals & signing

Sent, opened, signed — and you can prove it

A proposal goes out with options and attachments to a review page built for a homeowner on a phone. Delivery is tracked, so an ambiguous or failed send gets reconciled instead of firing a second email at your customer.

  • Proposal options the customer can choose between
  • Proposal templates and attachments
  • Customer-facing review page for homeowners
  • Acceptance and signature evidence stored on the record
  • Send history tracked per proposal
  • Failed or ambiguous delivery reconciled, never blind-resent

06 · Activity & notifications

A record you can settle an argument with

Lead events, status changes, proposal actions, operational events and system automations all write to one central timeline. When somebody asks what happened on a job, you look instead of guess.

Central activity timeline

Permanent records of the actions that matter, across leads, customers, projects and proposals.

Automatic event capture

Important user actions and system automations generate activity records without anybody remembering to log them.

Team notifications

Notification rules and delivery so the right people hear about the events that need a response — including through Slack.

07 · Team & workspace

Four seats, one set of records

Every company runs inside its own isolated workspace, with roles that decide what each person can reach.

Owner

Workspace, team, integrations, business settings and account lifecycle.

Admin / Manager

Leads, customers, jobs, proposals, integrations and team workflow.

Sales

Leads, follow-ups, estimates, quotes and proposals.

Office

Customer records, documents, scheduling-related workflow and operations.

Team invites

Invite staff into the workspace and set their role; membership can be made active or inactive.

Business settings

Workspace identity, currency, and estimate and proposal settings live with the business.

Workspace isolation

Each company's data stays in its own workspace, with safeguards against any cross-workspace action.

08 · Data & controls

Your data, and a door that opens outward

Safeguards run underneath the product, and the controls that matter belong to the owner rather than a support queue.

Operational safeguards

  • Protection against duplicate processing
  • Protection against conflicting operations
  • Protection against unsafe retries
  • Protection against cross-workspace actions
  • Encrypted storage of integration credentials

Owner controls

  • Data export
  • Personal account closure
  • Workspace closure, which blocks future activity
  • Automatic shutdown of connected integrations on closure
  • Facebook Page disconnect and connection cleanup

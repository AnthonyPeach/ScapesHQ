The through-line

A lead never gets re-entered to become a job

Each stage hands the same record to the next one. Same address, same people, same history — from the ad click to the signature to the crew's last note.

01 · Intake

Lead lands

Facebook Lead Ads route into the right workspace by connected Page. Phone-ins and referrals get typed once, with source captured.

02 · Convert

Relationship forms

Client, contacts, company and property link into the structure the real customer actually has.

03 · Price

Estimate built

Sections, line items and templates produce scope and totals without a rebuild each time.

04 · Close

Homeowner signs

A review page built for the customer, with acceptance and signature evidence stored on the proposal.

Where it separates

Built around a yard, a homeowner and a crew

Generic CRMs were designed for people who sell software. These are the parts contractors actually get burned by.

Meta Lead Ads, native

Connect a Page once and leads route themselves. Connection health is monitored, and failed intake can be retried or replayed instead of vanishing.

Customers aren't one flat row

Clients, contacts, companies and properties are linked records — so a property manager with nine buildings is one company, not nine duplicates.

Proposals homeowners can sign

Options, templates and attachments delivered to a customer-facing review page, with acceptance and signature evidence stored on the proposal.
